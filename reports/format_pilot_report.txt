Early monthly Table 7
---------------------
April, May, and June 2025 genuinely share one nine-column structure: State,
Sector, Sl No, Project Name/Agency/Project Code, approval month, original/
revised/anticipated commissioning, original/revised/anticipated cost,
cumulative expenditure, and physical progress. They use N-prefixed source
project identifiers, multiline names, separate parenthesised agency/code lines,
repeated headers, and wrapped state/sector cells.

The shared extract_early_format handler uses detected Table 7 pages and table
cells, preserves source page/file lineage, and does not turn anticipated values
into revisions. A defect in wrapped-state continuation rows was fixed. Counts
reconcile to terminal source serials: April 1,670 (pages 43-268), May 1,637
(43-207), June 1,595 (41-229), each with zero serial gaps/duplicates. Source
samples at the beginning and end and across page boundaries passed. Full
10-record-per-file fixtures are still pending, therefore these are
PASS_WITH_WARNINGS and remain a release blocker under the requested gate.

Quarterly QPISR
---------------
QPISR_QR_1st_2025-26.pdf uses Table 7 on PDF pages 59-256 and totals 1,734
serials. Its nine-column layout is closely related to early monthly Table 7,
but the title defines "1st Quarter (April-June), 2025-26" and some revised dates
are represented as full dates. These rows are a quarter-end/quarter-report
snapshot and overlap projects in monthly reports. They must be stored as a
separate quarterly dataset with explicit reporting-period semantics; mapping
them to project_monthly.csv as June observations is not yet defensible. The
dedicated extract_quarterly_format route exists. Full-date preservation and the
full golden fixture remain pending.

Difficult-case findings
-----------------------
The generic line state machine is not validated merely by consecutive serials.
August 2025 record 2 visibly begins with prior-row parenthetical values/code;
September 2025 record 3 begins with the prior project code; January 2026 sampled
records 931 and 1276 contain preceding-row text/identifiers; May 2026 record
1490 and June 2026 record 923 are similarly polluted. These are page/record
boundary failures and invalidate name, identifier, and sometimes agency fields.
No incorrect extracted value was promoted to a golden fixture.

Identifier coverage

not guessed where source sampling is incomplete. Known boundary failures are
classified LAYOUT_EXTRACTION_FAILURE; unresolved cases remain UNKNOWN.

Status vocabulary
-----------------
PASS: all required checks pass.
PASS_WITH_WARNINGS: sampled/source checks pass but a stated gate remains.
FAIL: a reproducible extraction defect exists.
NOT_APPLICABLE: check does not apply.
NOT_YET_TESTED: evidence is insufficient.
