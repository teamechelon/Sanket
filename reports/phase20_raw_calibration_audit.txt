5. CALIBRATION METHODOLOGY
Brier score is mean squared score error. The prevalence-only reference predicts the evaluated sample prevalence for every row; Brier skill is 1 - model Brier / reference Brier. ECE reuses the existing ten fixed equal-width bins over [0,1], right-closed with zero included in the first bin; empty bins remain visible and contribute zero weight. Calibration gap is mean score minus observed event rate.
Intercept and slope are joint diagnostic logistic-regression coefficients of the binary outcome on logit(raw score), with scores clipped only at fixed 1e-6 bounds for a finite diagnostic. These coefficients are never applied to scores and no calibrator or transformed predictions are created.
Discrimination asks whether ranking separates events; calibration asks whether score levels match event frequencies. Phase 16 PR-AUC/ROC-AUC are not evidence of probability calibration.
